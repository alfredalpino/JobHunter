## Personal narrative

JobHunter is an open-source tool I built while navigating real job markets — especially when freshness and fit matter more than volume. I was tired of boards full of stale reposts, “AI job tools” that auto-apply without care, and hours lost scrolling instead of applying well.

This is the product I wished existed: start from your resume, cut the noise, surface only recent roles that actually fit, and leave every application decision in your hands. **No auto-apply. Ever.**

I ship it as a browser app anyone can run, with an optional Python CLI for deeper hunts. The logic is honest and deterministic — AI is optional polish, not the engine.

---

## One-line truth

JobHunter is not a job board. It is a **resume-first filter** that turns noise into a short list of fresh roles you still choose to apply to yourself.

---

## Who it is for

**Primary:** Serious job aspirants — often relocating or switching markets (Dubai, USA, India, remote, worldwide) — who want fewer better matches, fresh posts only, and full control over every application.

**Secondary:** Power users who want the same workflow via CLI and region packs.

Not for recruiters, employers, or “apply to 500 jobs overnight” workflows.

## Brand values

| Value | In the product |
| --- | --- |
| **Freshness** | Default recency ≤14 days; buckets for last 7 days and 8–14 days |
| **Fit over volume** | Eligibility scoring, seniority guardrails, exclude keywords |
| **Human control** | No auto-apply, ever |
| **Honesty** | Cheap deterministic filters; optional AI polish only |
| **Clarity** | One linear hunt path, plain-English preferences |
| **Craft** | Open source, built by a maker, tool-not-platform |

---

## Personality

- **Focused** — late-night prep, not a carnival job fair
- **Calm but decisive** — dark atmosphere, glass UI, one clear next step
- **Premium tool** — editorial serif headlines, clean sans UI
- **Trustworthy** — no “AI will get you hired” hype
- **Global but personal** — works worldwide, feels built for one person’s hunt

**Tone:** precise, fresh, restrained, capable, honest, yours

**Avoid:** rocket ships, handshake clipart, “HIRED!” badges, robot arms, LinkedIn-blue corporate SaaS, spammy growth-hack energy

---

## Product story (3 beats)

1. **You** — your resume is the source of truth
2. **The hunt** — the tool scans sources, keeps what fits and is fresh
3. **Your move** — you open, apply, track; the tool never crosses that line

## Logo brief

**Name:** JobHunter  
**Descriptor:** By Alfred Alpino

**Core idea:** Resume in → fresh matches out → you take the shot.

### Symbol directions

1. **Scope / crosshair + horizon** — refine current mark; precision hunt
2. **Compass + shortlist** — direction + curated path
3. **Resume + filter funnel** — literal, strong at small sizes
4. **Target on a timeline** — fresh + one right role

### Deliverables

- Mark only (favicon, app icon, GitHub avatar)
- Mark + wordmark (header)
- Monochrome for dark/light backgrounds

### Must work at

16×16 favicon · 48×48 header · 512×512 social / OG

### Color usage

- Primary: seafoam + copper on ink
- Alternate: sand on ink
- Do not introduce bright blues, reds, or generic startup purple gradients
